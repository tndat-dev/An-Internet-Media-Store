1​ Introduction
1.1​ Objective
This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the functional and
non-functional requirements of the AIMS (An Internet Media Store) system. The
purpose of this document is to provide a clear and complete description of what the
system shall do so that analysts, designers, developers, testers, and project stakeholders
can use it as a common reference throughout the software development process. The SRS
is written based on the capstone project problem statement and the requirement analysis
results produced by the team.
This document is intended for the following audiences:
-​ Lecturer and teaching assistants: to review and evaluate the correctness and
completeness of the requirement analysis.
-​ Project team members: to ensure a common understanding of the system
requirements and project scope.
-​ Designers and developers: to design and implement the system in accordance with
the specified requirements.
-​ Testers: to derive test cases and verify that the implemented software satisfies the
stated requirements.
This document focuses on the required behavior of the system and does not describe
implementation details or design solutions.

1.2​ Scope
AIMS (An Internet Media Store) is an e-commerce software system developed as a web
or desktop application for buying and selling physical media products. The current scope
of the system includes the management and sale of four product types: books,
newspapers, CDs, and DVDs. The system supports activities for different actors,
including customers, product managers, and administrators.
For customers, the system allows browsing products, searching and filtering products,
viewing product details, adding items to cart, updating cart contents, placing orders,
entering delivery information, paying for orders, viewing order information, and
canceling orders before approval. The system also calculates delivery fees, generates

2

invoice information, records transactions, and sends email notifications related to order
status and payment.
For product managers, the system supports product and inventory-related operations such
as adding, viewing, editing, deleting, or deactivating products according to business rules,
manually adjusting stock with recorded reasons, reviewing pending orders, approving or
rejecting orders, and querying product history records.
For administrators, the system supports account and role management functions such as
creating, viewing, deactivating, blocking, and unblocking user accounts, assigning roles,
triggering password reset processes, and logging sensitive administrative actions.
The system is expected to operate continuously with acceptable reliability and
performance. It shall support up to 1,000 simultaneous customers, operate continuously
for 300 hours without failure, recover within 1 hour after an incident, and provide a
maximum response time of 2 seconds under normal conditions or 5 seconds during peak
hours.
At the current stage, the system is limited to physical media products and
demonstration-level payment integration using PayPal Sandbox and VietQR. Other
product categories, payment gateways, and notification methods may be supported in
future versions but are outside the current implementation scope.

2​ Overall Description
2.1​ Survey
AIMS provides a complete workflow for managing the lifecycle of physical media
products, from catalog management to customer transactions. The system integrates
internal operations such as inventory and account management with external services
such as payment gateways.
It supports customer interactions such as browsing, searching, filtering, and purchasing
products, while also enabling internal users to manage products, orders, and system
configurations. The system maintains logs and history records to ensure traceability and
support auditing.

2.2​ Overall requirements
2.2.1 Customer
-​ Browse Products:​
The customer browses the product catalog displayed by the system, including the
initial random product list shown when the application starts. The customer can
also search by title or category and filter by price range.
-​ View Product Detailed Information:​
The customer views the full information of a selected product, including general
information and product-type-specific details. From this screen, the customer may
also add the product to the cart.
-​ Check Cart:​
The customer reviews products currently added to the cart, including quantity,
product price, and total price excluding VAT. During this process, the customer
may change quantities or remove products from the cart. If stock is insufficient,
the system shall notify the customer.
-​ Place Order:​
The customer proceeds from the cart to checkout. The system checks stock
availability, requires delivery information, calculates shipping fees, generates
invoice information, and then continues to payment.
-​ Pay Order:​
The customer completes payment using the default QR-based payment method or
an alternative payment method through the PayPal gateway. After successful
payment, the system records the transaction, sets the order to Pending Processing,
and sends invoice and transaction information to the customer’s email.
-​ View Order Info:​
The customer can later access order details through the link sent by email. The
displayed information includes invoice details, shipping information, and payment
transaction information.
-​ Cancel Order:​
The customer may cancel an order before it is approved. If cancellation is
accepted, the paid amount is refunded to the original payment method according
to the refund rules of the payment gateway.
2.2.2 Product Manager
Product Manager must log in to access the management features corresponding to
their roles

7

-​ Manage Products:​
The Product Manager manages the lifecycle of physical media products in the
catalog. This includes creating new products, updating existing products, deleting
products when stock is zero, and changing product status to deactivated when
deletion is not allowed. The system also stores product addition, editing, and
deletion history, which can be queried by the Product Manager.
-​ Adjust Stock:​
The Product Manager manually adjusts stock quantity when necessary, for
example when receiving new inventory or removing damaged items. The system
requires a reason to be recorded for each manual stock change.
-​ Check Order:​
The Product Manager reviews orders in Pending Processing state. The system
displays pending orders and their details for inspection.
-​ Approve Order / Reject Order:​
After checking a pending order, the Product Manager may approve or reject it. If
an order is rejected, the system notifies the customer and handles refund support
according to the payment method.
2.2.3 Administrator
-​ Manage Users:​
The Administrator manages internal user accounts and roles, and must log in to
access the management features corresponding to their roles. This includes
creating accounts, viewing account information, deactivating accounts, blocking
or unblocking users, assigning roles, and triggering password reset processes.
Sensitive actions such as email updates and password resets must be logged, and
affected users must be notified.

2.3​ Business process
2.3.1 Purchasing Process
-​ Step 1: The Customer uses Browse Products to find suitable items and may use
View Product Detailed Information to inspect the details of a selected product.

8

-​ Step 2: The Customer adds desired products to the cart and uses Check Cart to
review selected items. During this process, the Customer may change quantities
or remove products if necessary.
-​ Step 3: The Customer selects Place Order. The system checks whether stock is
sufficient for the requested quantities. If stock is not sufficient, the Customer must
update the cart before continuing.
-​ Step 4: If stock is sufficient, the system requires the Customer to provide delivery
information. Each time the Customer enters or changes the delivery address, the
system calculates or recalculates the shipping fee and updates the invoice
information.
-​ Step 5: The Customer continues with Pay Order by using the default QR code
payment method or the alternative PayPal-based payment method. After
successful payment, the system records the transaction, creates the order in
Pending Processing state, and sends invoice and transaction information to the
Customer’s email.
-​ Step 6: After the order is created, the Customer may later use the email link to
View Order Info or perform Cancel Order before the order is approved.
2.3.2 Order Review Process
-​ Step 1: The Product Manager performs Login to access the management portal.
-​ Step 2: The Product Manager uses Check Order to retrieve pending orders and
review detailed order information.
-​ Step 3: Based on stock conditions and business constraints, the Product Manager
decides to Approve Order or Reject Order.
-​ Step 4: If the order is approved or rejected, the system sends a notification email
to the customer. If the order is rejected, refund handling is performed according to
the payment method capabilities.
2.3.3 Product Management Process
-​ Step 1: The Product Manager performs Login to access product management
functions.
-​ Step 2: The Product Manager uses Manage Products to create new products,
update existing products, or delete selected products according to product rules.
-​ Step 3: If a selected product has stock greater than zero, the system does not
delete it. Instead, the product status is changed to Deactivated so it is no longer
available for sale.

9

-​ Step 4: Whenever stock is manually changed, the Product Manager uses Adjust
Stock and provides a reason for the adjustment. The system records this change
for traceability.
-​ Step 5: The system stores product addition, editing, and deletion history, and the
Product Manager may query these records when needed.
2.3.4 User Management Process
-​ Step 1: The Administrator performs Login to access account management
functions.
-​ Step 2: The Administrator uses Manage Users to create accounts, assign roles,
deactivate users, or block and unblock accounts.
-​ Step 3: If password reset or another sensitive account action is required, the
Administrator triggers the action and the system logs it and sends notification to
the affected user.

3.2​ Use case 2

Use Case “Pay Order”
1.​ Use case code
UC002

2.​ Brief Description
This use case describes the interactions between the Customer, AIMS software,
and VietQR when the customer desires to pay for a placed order using a QR code.
AIMS software generates a VietQR QR code for the customer to scan and
complete the payment via their banking application. VietQR then sends a payment
callback to AIMS software to confirm the transaction result. This use case is

16

included by the use case “Place Order”. If the customer does not wish to pay by
QR code, they may switch to credit card payment, which triggers the use case
“Pay Order via PayPal”.

3.​ Actors
3.1​ Customer
The customer is the end-user who scans the QR code using their banking application to
complete the payment, or who may choose to switch to credit card payment via PayPal.

3.2​

VietQR

VietQR is an external payment service provider. AIMS software calls VietQR’s API to
generate the QR code. VietQR then sends a payment callback to AIMS software with the
transaction result.

4.​ Preconditions
●​ The use case “Place Order” has been initiated and the customer has confirmed the
invoice.
●​ The invoice information — including the list of products in the cart, quantities,
product prices, total product price excluding VAT, total product price including VAT,
delivery fee (not subject to VAT), and total amount to be paid — has been calculated
and temporarily saved by AIMS software.
●​ AIMS software has valid VietQR API credentials configured.

5.​ Basic Flow of Events
Step 1. AIMS software calls the VietQR API to generate a QR code, providing the store’s
bank account information, the total amount to be paid (from the temporarily saved
invoice), and the transfer content corresponding to the order. (See Table B – Input data for
QR code generation.)
Step 2. VietQR returns the QR code to AIMS software.
Step 3. AIMS software displays the payment screen to the customer, showing: the QR
code; bank name and account number of the receiving account; transfer content; and the
full invoice information including product list (title, quantity, unit price, amount per line),
total product price excluding VAT, total product price including VAT, delivery fee (not
subject to VAT), and total amount to be paid. (See Table A – Output data of payment
screen.) The customer may also choose to switch to credit card payment via PayPal at this
step.
Step 4. Customer scans the QR code using their banking application and completes the
transfer. (Note: In the test/sandbox environment, the customer triggers VietQR’s test
callback API to simulate a successful payment transaction.)

17

Step 5. VietQR sends a payment callback to AIMS software with the transaction result,
including: transaction ID, transaction content, transaction datetime, confirmed amount,
and status. (See Table C – Input data from VietQR payment callback.)
Step 6. AIMS software verifies that the callback status is “success” and that the confirmed
amount matches the total amount on the invoice.

4​ Supplementary specification
4.1 Functionality
-​ Authentication & Access: Administrators and product managers must log in to
access role-specific features. Administrators are responsible for managing user
accounts in accordance with the principle of least privilege and data protection
standards. Administrators may trigger password reset processes but cannot access
users' actual passwords, as all passwords are securely hashed. For customers, no
login is required.
-​ Auditing & Logging: The software will store the history of products addition,
editing and deletion. Product managers can query these histories whenever they
want. Manual stock adjustments must be performed by the product manager, and
the reason for the stock change must be explicitly recorded. The software records
the payment transaction information and the successfully paid order. Any
sensitive administrative actions, such as email updates or password resets, must
be logged.
-​ Notifications: Any sensitive administrative actions must be automatically notified
to the affected users. The software will notify the product manager if any
operation is invalid. The software will send invoice and payment transaction
information to the customer's email. Once an order is either accepted or rejected, a
notification email will be sent to the customer who placed the order. If an order
paid via VietQR is rejected, the system will notify the Product Manager that a
manual refund to the customer is required for this payment method.
-​ System Constraints: The price of the product must always be between 30% and
150% of the product's original value to avoid price inflation or undercutting. For
security reasons, managers cannot delete more than twenty products per day. A
product can only be deleted if its stock is equal to zero, otherwise it cannot be
deleted; instead, its status will be changed to "deactivated".

4.2 Usability
-​ Ease of Use: The e-commerce software operates 24/7 allowing new users to easily
familiarize themselves.
-​ Navigation Flexibility: Customers can go back to any step or exit the software
during the ordering process.

4.3 Reliability
-​ Availability: The software operates 24/7.
-​ Fault Tolerance: This software can operate continuously for 300 hours without
failure.

39

-​ Recoverability: The software can resume normal operation within a maximum of
1 hour after an incident.

4.4 Performance
-​ Capacity: This software can serve up to 1,000 customers simultaneously without
significantly reducing performance.
-​ Response Time: The maximum response time of the software is 2 seconds under
normal conditions or 5 seconds during peak hours.

4.5 Supportability
-​ Extensibility for Products: Currently, AIMS supports only the buying and selling
of physical media products, including books, newspapers, CDs, and DVDs (other
types of products may be supported in the future).
-​ Extensibility for Services: Other payment gateways may be supported in the
future. In the future, additional notification methods such as SMS or push
notifications may also be supported.

4.6 Other requirements
-​ Platform: AIMS is an e-commerce software (web or desktop application).
-​ Integration: Users will integrate payments using PayPal Sandbox and VietQR (via
QR code). Currently, since only PayPal supports the refund API, the system does
not handle refunds for VietQR payments. In such cases, the Product Manager is
responsible for issuing refunds manually (e.g., contacting the customer and
transferring funds outside the system).
